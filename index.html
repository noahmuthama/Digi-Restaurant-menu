How it all works

  1. Choose the preferred dish and enter table number

  3. You will see the waiter on duty to serve
  4. The waiter will give an approximate wait time
  5. you will see the wait time
  6. The waiter will arrive with your food
  7. after the timer is over it just displays the receipt with pay button
  8. when you pay you can add a feedback(optional)
  9. The waiter sees and comes collect dishes or any free waiter
